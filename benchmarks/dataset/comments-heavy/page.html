How to Cold Brew Coffee

Cold brew is coffee steeped in cold water for a long time instead of being brewed with heat. The slow extraction pulls out sweetness and body while leaving behind much of the acidity and bitterness you get from a hot brew, which is why cold brew tastes so smooth.

Start with a coarse grind, about the texture of raw sugar. Combine one part coffee to eight parts cold water in a jar, stir once to wet all the grounds, and refrigerate for twelve to eighteen hours. Strain through a paper filter to remove the fine sediment.

The result is a concentrate. Dilute it one to one with water or milk over ice. Stored in a sealed container in the fridge, the concentrate keeps its flavour for about a week.
