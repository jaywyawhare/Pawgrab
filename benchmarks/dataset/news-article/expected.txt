City Approves New Bike Lane Network

By Jordan Ellis · March 3, 2024

The city council voted on Tuesday to approve a forty-kilometre network of protected bike lanes, the largest cycling infrastructure investment in the city's history. The plan passed by a margin of nine votes to four after a lengthy public comment period.

Construction will begin in the spring and roll out in three phases over two years. The first phase connects the downtown core to the university district, corridors that currently carry the heaviest cycling traffic and the highest rate of collisions.

Supporters argued the lanes will make commuting safer and reduce congestion, while some business owners raised concerns about the loss of on-street parking. The council set aside funds for a study to measure the impact on local retail after the first year.
